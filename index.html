§ 01 Practice

I am a South African architectural technologist with twelve years of experience spanning private-sector commercial work and large-scale public infrastructure. As Programme Manager (FTCW) at PRASA CRES in the South Gauteng Region, I currently direct the revival of vandalized train stations and multiple refurbishment programmes across critical transport corridors.

Before joining PRASA, I spent seven years as a Project Architect at management level with Koen & Associates Architects in Sandton, leading concept-to-completion delivery on retail centres, corporate offices, and industrial parks. I began my career at Stauch Vorster Architects in Illovo, where I trained as an architectural technologist across a broad portfolio of commercial buildings.

I hold Bachelors, Honours, and Master of Architecture (Professional) degrees from the University of the Witwatersrand, and am a candidate member of SACAP (CANT 65848713, PPE-registered). In 2022–23 I also served a term as Non-Executive Director on the board of the Johannesburg Property Company.
